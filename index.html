About me

I am a master's student in Health Data Science at Juntendo University, working in the Himeno-Sun Lab under the supervision of Prof. Ryutaro Himeno and Prof. Zhe Sun at Juntendo, and Dr. Chao Li at RIKEN AIP.

As part of the Brain/MINDS 2.0 Digital Brain Project, I adapt Transformer-based sequence models to rat neural recordings and ask how well what they learn carries over across trials, recording sessions and animals. I am interested in neural population dynamics and coding, and in computational models of cognition and behavior.

Before Juntendo, I received a BSc (Hons) in Information and Computing Science from Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, a dual degree with the University of Liverpool.
